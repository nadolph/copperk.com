About Kelvin Adolph

"I believe that selling your home shouldn't cost you a fortune. My mission is to provide top-tier real estate services while allowing you to keep more of your hard-earned equity."

Kelvin Adolph is the Broker and owner of Copper K Realty (K Adolph Realty Corporation). With over 25 years of experience in the real estate industry, he has witnessed the evolution of the market and the varying needs of clients across Calgary and surrounding areas.

Unlike large "team" brokerages where you might be passed off to a junior agent, Kelvin prides himself on providing direct, personal service. When you hire Copper K Realty, you get Kelvin Adolph.

Experience Matters

Navigating the complexities of real estate transactions requires a steady hand and deep knowledge. Kelvin has successfully brokered hundreds of millions of dollars in real estate, handling everything from first-time home purchases to complex rural property sales.

His expertise covers:

  • Residential Real Estate (Detached, Attached, Condos)
  • Rural and Acreage Properties
  • Market Analysis and Property Valuation
  • Strategic Negotiation
